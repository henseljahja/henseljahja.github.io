Research · Universitas Brawijaya

Preprint on arXiv

arXiv:2203.11542 · cs.CV · preprint, Mar 2022

Mask-usage recognition with a Vision Transformer

Classifies whether a mask is worn correctly across four classes, trained on MaskedFace-Net's 137.016 images. I compared ViT Base-16, Large-16 and Huge-14 against ResNet, with and without transfer learning from ImageNet-21k and random augmentation.

The Grad-CAM maps show what each model looks at: ViT focuses on the mask itself, while ResNet152 drifts to the cheeks and hair.

Best model
ViT Huge-14 with transfer learning and augmentation
Test accuracy
95,3% for ViT Huge-14 vs 55,3% for ResNet-50
Authors
Hensel D. Jahja (first author), Novanto Yudistira, Sutrisno
Stack
Vision Transformer · Grad-CAM
